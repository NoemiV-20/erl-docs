eSpark's Reading Lab

Comprehensive Phonics Scope & Sequence

Discover the power of eSpark's Reading Lab (eRL), a Science of Reading aligned personalized phonics program designed to build confident, proficient readers. Our systematic and explicit phonics instruction ensures students master foundational skills at every step of their learning journey. Explore our comprehensive scope and sequence below.

Kindergarten: Building the Foundation

Unit 1: Letters, Consonants & Short Vowels

Lesson 78: -ck as /k/ pick, quick This lesson introduces the final consonant digraph '-ck' which produces the /k/ sound. Students will decode words such as 'pick' and 'quick', learning this common ending for the /k/ sound after a short vowel.

Lesson 81: Digraph Pattern Exception -all, a as /au/ fall This lesson introduces a digraph pattern exception with '-all', where 'a' makes an /au/ (or /aul/) sound, not its typical short sound. Students will practice with words like 'fall'.
Lesson 82: Digraph Pattern Exceptions -ull & -ush, u as Short /o͝o/ pull, push This lesson introduces digraph pattern exceptions with '-ull' (as in 'pull') and '-ush' (as in 'push'), where 'u' makes a short /o͝o/ sound (like in 'book'). Students will practice these /o͝ol/ and /o͝osh/ sounds.
Lesson 83: Cumulative Digraphs with Short Vowels king, pink, shop, shrimp, chip, lunch, match, this, bath, thrill, whiz, pick, huff, bell, fall, pull This lesson is a cumulative assessment of various consonant digraphs combined with short vowels.
