Where the Science of Reading Meets Personalized Phonics Instruction!

The eSpark Reading Lab (eRL), a key feature of eSpark's comprehensive Phonics scope and sequence, ignites a passion for reading through a dynamic, explicit phonics journey that is firmly rooted in the core principles of the Science of Reading. This personalized, research-backed instruction is designed to empower students to master foundational skills and confidently navigate the path to reading proficiency and complex literacy. Unit 1: Letters, Consonants & Short Vowels
